# Imbewu: Architecture and Evidence

The pitch-ready architecture document (part 1 of the pitch package) is kept
as a shared, editable document so it can be commented on and exported to
Word or PDF for funders:

https://claude.ai/artifact/TCNs8s4n2NNEMXQE2Lnu15

First version: 2026-11-24. Sections: summary; why Imbewu and why Africa;
the five design goals and their status; architecture at a glance (diagram);
the compute engine; the tile; the vector unit; software; results (whole-
model accuracy chart, performance model, physical estimates); how the
design was checked; what simulation proves and what it does not; what is
needed next (roadmap diagram, people, partners, licensed parts, funding);
glossary.

Every number in it comes from this repository: `docs/RESULTS.md`, the
`results/` files and the decisions in `DECISIONS.md`. When results change,
update the document too.
